San Francisco Rush 2049: Recompiled v0.1.28: Scenery Culling has a new Reduced setting, for weaker PCs that lose frames with it Off.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

You need your own ROM of the US release (`.z64`, SHA-1 `3f99351d7bb61656614bdb2aa1a90cfe55d1922c`); the launcher finds it by itself in a usual place (the game's folder, Downloads, Desktop, Documents and one level of subfolders), otherwise pick it with **Load ROM**.

**Windows:** unzip `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.28-windows-x64.zip` and run `Rush2049Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.28-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Rush2049Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType). Settings and saves carry over from earlier versions.

**New:** **Scenery Culling** (Graphics tab) has a third value, **Reduced**, between Original and Off. Off draws all of a track's scenery wherever you are, which ends the scenery popping in and out close to the car but can cost a weaker PC a quarter of its frames (in the attract race on a busy 6-core PC, 60 fps dropped to 45). Reduced draws what can be seen from every stretch of road near you, so scenery comes in well ahead of the car, for about a quarter more drawing than Original instead of Off's two and a half times (59 fps there). Off stays the default; on a weaker PC, try Reduced before Original.

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/rush-2049-recomp#readme) has the controls, every setting, and how to build it yourself.
