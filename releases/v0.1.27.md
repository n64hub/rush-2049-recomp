San Francisco Rush 2049: Recompiled v0.1.27: Draw Distance, Level of Detail, Scenery Culling and Controllers apply their defaults from the very first launch.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

You need your own ROM of the US release (`.z64`, SHA-1 `3f99351d7bb61656614bdb2aa1a90cfe55d1922c`); the launcher finds it by itself in a usual place (the game's folder, Downloads, Desktop, Documents and one level of subfolders), otherwise pick it with **Load ROM**.

**Windows:** unzip `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.27-windows-x64.zip` and run `Rush2049Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.27-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Rush2049Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType). Settings and saves carry over from earlier versions.

**Fixed:** on the very first launch, before a settings file existed, four settings ran at other values than the menu showed until the game was restarted: Draw Distance and Level of Detail at Original instead of 4×, **Scenery Culling** at Original instead of Off (so v0.1.26's fix for scenery popping in and out only took effect from the second launch), and Controllers at One Player instead of Per Player. They now apply their defaults from the start. Settings and saves carry over.

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/rush-2049-recomp#readme) has the controls, every setting, and how to build it yourself.
