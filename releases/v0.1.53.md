San Francisco Rush 2049: Recompiled v0.1.53: a ghost's arrow is no longer covered by buildings behind the ghost.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

You need your own ROM of the US release (`.z64`, SHA-1 `3f99351d7bb61656614bdb2aa1a90cfe55d1922c`); the launcher finds it by itself in a usual place (the game's folder, Downloads, Desktop, Documents and one level of subfolders), otherwise pick it with **Load ROM**.

**Windows:** unzip `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.53-windows-x64.zip` and run `Rush2049Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.53-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Rush2049Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType). Settings and saves carry over from earlier versions.

**Fixed:** with **Ghost Arrows** at **Whole** (the default), the arrow over a ghost was still covered by scenery behind the ghost, however far away it was: on Track 1 the buildings beside the straight after the Slim Jim sign, and the signal arm on the bridge at its end, cut into the arrows of ghosts in plain view. The game draws the arrows before part of the scenery, and the arrows don't count for what is in front, so that scenery was painted over them. Whole now draws the arrows after all the scenery, each one whole while its tip above the ghost can be seen, and not at all while something in front of it hides the tip. **Original** draws them as the game did.

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/rush-2049-recomp#readme) has the controls, every setting, and how to build it yourself.
