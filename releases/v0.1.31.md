San Francisco Rush 2049: Recompiled v0.1.31: a tire squeal no longer gets stuck after Rewind.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

You need your own ROM of the US release (`.z64`, SHA-1 `3f99351d7bb61656614bdb2aa1a90cfe55d1922c`); the launcher finds it by itself in a usual place (the game's folder, Downloads, Desktop, Documents and one level of subfolders), otherwise pick it with **Load ROM**.

**Windows:** unzip `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.31-windows-x64.zip` and run `Rush2049Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.31-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Rush2049Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType). Settings and saves carry over from earlier versions.

**Fixed:** a tire squeal that started while Rewind was held could keep playing after you let go, for the rest of the race and on into the menus. The squeal is a sound the race stops itself, and the race that stops it was rewound away, so nothing ever did. Letting go of Rewind now ends every sound started in the stretch that was rewound, the squeal too, not only the looping ones. A car's engine sound also follows the rewound race's speed at every step back, where before it could stay as loud as it was before you rewound.

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/rush-2049-recomp#readme) has the controls, every setting, and how to build it yourself.
