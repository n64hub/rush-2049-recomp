San Francisco Rush 2049: Recompiled v0.1.31: scenery out of view is no longer drawn, so Scenery Culling Off costs far fewer frames.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

You need your own ROM of the US release (`.z64`, SHA-1 `3f99351d7bb61656614bdb2aa1a90cfe55d1922c`); the launcher finds it by itself in a usual place (the game's folder, Downloads, Desktop, Documents and one level of subfolders), otherwise pick it with **Load ROM**.

**Windows:** unzip `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.31-windows-x64.zip` and run `Rush2049Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.31-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Rush2049Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType). Settings and saves carry over from earlier versions.

**Faster:** the game gives every piece of scenery a box around it, so that the N64 can skip whatever is wholly behind the camera or off the sides of the screen, but the renderer drew it all anyway. It now skips it too, on the widened picture of a widescreen window and with a margin at every edge, so nothing at the edges goes missing. With **Scenery Culling** at **Off** (the default) that halves the drawing: in the attract race on a busy 6-core PC, 1960 draws a frame become 840 and 43 fps becomes 58, which is about what Reduced cost before. Original and Reduced draw less too (490 and 570 draws, 60 and 59 fps there). If Off cost you frames before, try it again; Reduced is still there for a PC that needs it. Nothing to set.

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/rush-2049-recomp#readme) has the controls, every setting, and how to build it yourself.
