San Francisco Rush 2049: Recompiled v0.1.29: Rewind works properly in a Ghost Race.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

You need your own ROM of the US release (`.z64`, SHA-1 `3f99351d7bb61656614bdb2aa1a90cfe55d1922c`); the launcher finds it by itself in a usual place (the game's folder, Downloads, Desktop, Documents and one level of subfolders), otherwise pick it with **Load ROM**.

**Windows:** unzip `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.29-windows-x64.zip` and run `Rush2049Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.29-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Rush2049Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType). Settings and saves carry over from earlier versions.

**Fixed:** **Rewind** in a Ghost Race:
- While Rewind is held, a Ghost Race's countdown counts back up with the race instead of counting down.
- Rewinding to the start of a race no longer shows a garbled box of text from the menus, and the radar on the left no longer flickers while rewinding.
- Rewinding back over the start line just after a lap, and driving over it again, now counts that lap's real time. Before, it counted (and showed) a lap of a few milliseconds.

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/rush-2049-recomp#readme) has the controls, every setting, and how to build it yourself.
