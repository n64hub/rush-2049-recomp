San Francisco Rush 2049: Recompiled v0.1.30: the renderer and runtime brought up to date.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

You need your own ROM of the US release (`.z64`, SHA-1 `3f99351d7bb61656614bdb2aa1a90cfe55d1922c`); the launcher finds it by itself in a usual place (the game's folder, Downloads, Desktop, Documents and one level of subfolders), otherwise pick it with **Load ROM**.

**Windows:** unzip `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.30-windows-x64.zip` and run `Rush2049Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.30-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Rush2049Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType). Settings and saves carry over from earlier versions.

**Changed:** the renderer and the runtime under the game are the newest ones the web version runs. Nothing you should notice in a race: the renderer's alpha test now keeps every pixel a triangle covers where a game asks for coverage, as a console does, and on Linux a clipping rectangle past the window's edge no longer trips Vulkan drivers that check it. Nothing to set: settings and saves are as before.

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/rush-2049-recomp#readme) has the controls, every setting, and how to build it yourself.
