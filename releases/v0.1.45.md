San Francisco Rush 2049: Recompiled v0.1.45: Reduced Scenery Culling shows buildings standing high above you when you drive the wrong way.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

You need your own ROM of the US release (`.z64`, SHA-1 `3f99351d7bb61656614bdb2aa1a90cfe55d1922c`); the launcher finds it by itself in a usual place (the game's folder, Downloads, Desktop, Documents and one level of subfolders), otherwise pick it with **Load ROM**.

**Windows:** unzip `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.45-windows-x64.zip` and run `Rush2049Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.45-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Rush2049Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType). Settings and saves carry over from earlier versions.

**Fixed:** with Scenery Culling at Reduced, buildings that stand on higher ground no longer pop in when you drive a track backward. On Track 1, coming out of the tunnel onto the start straight the wrong way, a group of buildings on the hill ahead on the left came in all at once. They are shorter than the towers v0.1.39 already let through, but stand high above the road. Reduced now also draws any building whose top stands well above you, wherever it is in view, so it comes in while it is still far off. In the attract race this costs about one more object in view and no measurable frame time.

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/rush-2049-recomp#readme) has the controls, every setting, and how to build it yourself.
