San Francisco Rush 2049: Recompiled v0.1.44: a ghost's arrow is drawn whole while the ghost can be seen.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

You need your own ROM of the US release (`.z64`, SHA-1 `3f99351d7bb61656614bdb2aa1a90cfe55d1922c`); the launcher finds it by itself in a usual place (the game's folder, Downloads, Desktop, Documents and one level of subfolders), otherwise pick it with **Load ROM**.

**Windows:** unzip `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.44-windows-x64.zip` and run `Rush2049Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.44-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Rush2049Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType). Settings and saves carry over from earlier versions.

**New:** **Ghost Arrows** (Graphics tab). The arrow over a ghost was drawn into the scene, so anything nearer covered part of it: a ghost driving under the deck beside Track 1's footbridge straight lost the top of its arrow while in plain view. At **Whole**, the default, an arrow is drawn whole, in front of everything, while its tip above the ghost can be seen, and not at all while something hides the tip, so arrows still don't show through walls. **Original** draws them as the game did.

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/rush-2049-recomp#readme) has the controls, every setting, and how to build it yourself.
