San Francisco Rush 2049: Recompiled v0.1.80: the launcher takes the ROM packed in an archive.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

You need your own ROM of the US release (`.z64`, SHA-1 `3f99351d7bb61656614bdb2aa1a90cfe55d1922c`), as it is or packed in a `.zip`, `.7z`, `.gz`, `.xz` or `.tar`; the launcher finds it by itself in a usual place (the game's folder, Downloads, Desktop, Documents and one level of subfolders), otherwise pick it, or the archive holding it, with **Load ROM**.

**Windows:** unzip `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.80-windows-x64.zip` and run `Rush2049Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.80-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Rush2049Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType). Settings and saves carry over from earlier versions.

**Changed:** The launcher takes the ROM packed in a `.zip`, `.7z`, `.gz`, `.xz` or `.tar` (a `.tar.gz` or `.tar.xz` too): pick the archive with **Load ROM**, or leave it where the launcher looks by itself, and it finds the ROM inside. Before, it said an archive was not a valid ROM file.

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/rush-2049-recomp#readme) has the controls, every setting, and how to build it yourself.
