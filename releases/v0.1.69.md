San Francisco Rush 2049: Recompiled v0.1.69: Single Race and Practice for 3 and 4 players.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

You need your own ROM of the US release (`.z64`, SHA-1 `3f99351d7bb61656614bdb2aa1a90cfe55d1922c`); the launcher finds it by itself in a usual place (the game's folder, Downloads, Desktop, Documents and one level of subfolders), otherwise pick it with **Load ROM**.

**Windows:** unzip `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.69-windows-x64.zip` and run `Rush2049Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.69-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Rush2049Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType). Settings and saves carry over from earlier versions.

**New:** Single Race and Practice for 3 and 4 players, each in a quarter of the screen, with computer cars racing them (**Race Players** on the General tab, Up to 4 by default). 4 players see the 64-pixel track map 1 to 3 players see (**Split-Screen Map**, Graphics), and a 4-player race holds 60 fps on a desktop PC. A player's race position stays on the HUD after a crash (**Race Position**, General). Contributed by @IZIDIOZZ (#357).

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/rush-2049-recomp#readme) has the controls, every setting, and how to build it yourself.
