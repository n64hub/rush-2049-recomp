San Francisco Rush 2049: Recompiled v0.1.46: sound effects keep playing while Rewind is held and after you let go.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

You need your own ROM of the US release (`.z64`, SHA-1 `3f99351d7bb61656614bdb2aa1a90cfe55d1922c`); the launcher finds it by itself in a usual place (the game's folder, Downloads, Desktop, Documents and one level of subfolders), otherwise pick it with **Load ROM**.

**Windows:** unzip `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.46-windows-x64.zip` and run `Rush2049Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.46-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Rush2049Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType). Settings and saves carry over from earlier versions.
**Fixed:** sound effects no longer go silent while Rewind is held, or after you let go. The game keeps each car's ongoing sounds (the engine, the tire squeal, the grass or dirt under the wheels and the grind against a wall) by a number its sound player hands out. Going back to a moment when one of them played gave the race back the number of a sound that had already ended, so it stayed silent until the race would have stopped it itself: the squeal or grind went quiet partway through a rewind, the engine went quiet after rewinding back past a crash, and letting go during a grind left it silent for as long as you stayed on the wall. Every frame Rewind steps back now starts any such sound again, and it plays on through the rest of the rewind and after you let go.

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/rush-2049-recomp#readme) has the controls, every setting, and how to build it yourself.
