San Francisco Rush 2049: Recompiled v0.1.36: no more stuck sounds while Rewind is held, and engines play on after it.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

You need your own ROM of the US release (`.z64`, SHA-1 `3f99351d7bb61656614bdb2aa1a90cfe55d1922c`); the launcher finds it by itself in a usual place (the game's folder, Downloads, Desktop, Documents and one level of subfolders), otherwise pick it with **Load ROM**.

**Windows:** unzip `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.36-windows-x64.zip` and run `Rush2049Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.36-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Rush2049Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType). Settings and saves carry over from earlier versions.

**Fixed:** while Rewind was held, the tire squeal of each skid it went back over, and the sound of driving on dirt, kept playing until you let go. Every frame Rewind steps back now ends the sounds the race no longer knows about, so they stop as soon as the race goes back past them.

**Fixed:** after rewinding past a crash, a car's engine could go silent until you paused and unpaused. The crash had stopped the engine and the respawn started a new one, and Rewind took the race back to the stopped one. When you let go of Rewind, a car whose engine sound is gone now has it started again, the way unpausing does.

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/rush-2049-recomp#readme) has the controls, every setting, and how to build it yourself.
