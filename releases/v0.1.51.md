San Francisco Rush 2049: Recompiled v0.1.51: a Mouse Cursor setting that keeps the mouse cursor on screen while you play with a controller.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

You need your own ROM of the US release (`.z64`, SHA-1 `3f99351d7bb61656614bdb2aa1a90cfe55d1922c`); the launcher finds it by itself in a usual place (the game's folder, Downloads, Desktop, Documents and one level of subfolders), otherwise pick it with **Load ROM**.

**Windows:** unzip `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.51-windows-x64.zip` and run `Rush2049Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.51-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Rush2049Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType). Settings and saves carry over from earlier versions.

**New:** **Mouse Cursor** on the General tab. The cursor hides as soon as a controller or a key is pressed and comes back when the mouse moves; set it to **Always Shown** and it stays where you left it while you play, to point at things in a recording. Original, the default, hides it as before.

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/rush-2049-recomp#readme) has the controls, every setting, and how to build it yourself.
