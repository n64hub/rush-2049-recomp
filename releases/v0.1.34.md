San Francisco Rush 2049: Recompiled v0.1.34: the launcher has artwork and a title that fits, and the program has an icon.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

You need your own ROM of the US release (`.z64`, SHA-1 `3f99351d7bb61656614bdb2aa1a90cfe55d1922c`); the launcher finds it by itself in a usual place (the game's folder, Downloads, Desktop, Documents and one level of subfolders), otherwise pick it with **Load ROM**.

**Windows:** unzip `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.34-windows-x64.zip` and run `Rush2049Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.34-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Rush2049Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType). Settings and saves carry over from earlier versions.

**A launcher that looks the part:** instead of a black screen with the name in blue text that barely fit, the launcher shows a night San Francisco, with the bridge, the skyline and a road into the city, under a "Rush 2049" title that is sized to the window. The artwork is the project's own, not the game's logo or box art.

**An icon:** `Rush2049Recompiled.exe` has an icon in Explorer and on the taskbar, and the game's window shows it in its corner, on Linux too.

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/rush-2049-recomp#readme) has the controls, every setting, and how to build it yourself.
