San Francisco Rush 2049: Recompiled v0.1.54: Scenery Culling Reduced also draws buildings that look wide from where you are, so fewer pop in on Track 2.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

You need your own ROM of the US release (`.z64`, SHA-1 `3f99351d7bb61656614bdb2aa1a90cfe55d1922c`); the launcher finds it by itself in a usual place (the game's folder, Downloads, Desktop, Documents and one level of subfolders), otherwise pick it with **Load ROM**.

**Windows:** unzip `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.54-windows-x64.zip` and run `Rush2049Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.54-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Rush2049Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType). Settings and saves carry over from earlier versions.

**Fixed:** with **Scenery Culling** at **Reduced**, a long, low building in the valley on Track 2 driven the right way still popped in all at once just after the valley came into view: it stood far off and below the road, so it never looked tall enough for Reduced to draw it early, though it is wide. Reduced now also draws any building that looks big from where you are, counting its width as well as its height, so it is there well before the view opens. It draws about a tenth more than before and still far less than **Off**. **Original** and **Off** are unchanged.

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/rush-2049-recomp#readme) has the controls, every setting, and how to build it yourself.
