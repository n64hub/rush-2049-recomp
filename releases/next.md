**Changed:** **Ghost Arrows** (Graphics tab) has **After Scenery** in place of **Whole**, and it is the default: the arrows are still drawn after the scenery, so buildings far behind a ghost no longer cover its arrow, but now only what is between you and an arrow covers it, and only the part of it behind that. A sign or a bridge a ghost drives under no longer makes its whole arrow blink out, and a beam in front of an arrow is no longer drawn behind it. A setting saved as Whole loads as After Scenery.
