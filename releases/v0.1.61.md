San Francisco Rush 2049: Recompiled v0.1.61: ghost arrows are covered by what is in front of them again, part by part.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

You need your own ROM of the US release (`.z64`, SHA-1 `3f99351d7bb61656614bdb2aa1a90cfe55d1922c`); the launcher finds it by itself in a usual place (the game's folder, Downloads, Desktop, Documents and one level of subfolders), otherwise pick it with **Load ROM**.

**Windows:** unzip `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.61-windows-x64.zip` and run `Rush2049Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.61-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Rush2049Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType). Settings and saves carry over from earlier versions.

**Changed:** **Ghost Arrows** (Graphics tab) has **After Scenery** in place of **Whole**, and it is the default. The arrows are still drawn after the scenery, so buildings far behind a ghost no longer cover its arrow, but they are no longer shown or hidden whole by whether their tip can be seen: as in the original game, whatever is between you and an arrow covers only the part of it behind that. A setting saved as Whole loads as After Scenery.

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/rush-2049-recomp#readme) has the controls, every setting, and how to build it yourself.
