San Francisco Rush 2049: Recompiled v0.1.47: one-off sounds such as a crash or an explosion play while Rewind is held.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

You need your own ROM of the US release (`.z64`, SHA-1 `3f99351d7bb61656614bdb2aa1a90cfe55d1922c`); the launcher finds it by itself in a usual place (the game's folder, Downloads, Desktop, Documents and one level of subfolders), otherwise pick it with **Load ROM**.

**Windows:** unzip `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.47-windows-x64.zip` and run `Rush2049Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.47-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Rush2049Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType). Settings and saves carry over from earlier versions.

**Fixed:** one-off sounds, such as a crash, an explosion or a landing, play again while Rewind is held, once each as the rewind goes back past them, the way the music plays on. Since v0.1.36, every frame Rewind stepped back ended every sound the race no longer knew about, so the stuck tire squeal and dirt sounds would stop, and that ended these too before they could be heard. Now only the sounds that would otherwise play on are ended, and the engine, squeal, wheel and wall-grind sounds keep playing as in v0.1.46.

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/rush-2049-recomp#readme) has the controls, every setting, and how to build it yourself.
