San Francisco Rush 2049: Recompiled v0.1.26: buildings, walls and signs no longer pop in and out as you drive along a track.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

You need your own ROM of the US release (`.z64`, SHA-1 `3f99351d7bb61656614bdb2aa1a90cfe55d1922c`); the launcher finds it by itself in a usual place (the game's folder, Downloads, Desktop, Documents and one level of subfolders), otherwise pick it with **Load ROM**.

**Windows:** unzip `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.26-windows-x64.zip` and run `Rush2049Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Rush2049Recompiled-v0.1.26-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Rush2049Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType). Settings and saves carry over from earlier versions.

**New:** **Scenery Culling** (Graphics tab), set to **Off** by default: the whole track's scenery is drawn wherever you are. The game left out the scenery it reckoned couldn't be seen from the stretch of road you were on, so as you drove from one stretch into the next, buildings, walls and signs popped in and out, some of them a few car lengths away; Draw Distance couldn't help with that. **Original** keeps the game's culling. Settings and saves carry over.

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/rush-2049-recomp#readme) has the controls, every setting, and how to build it yourself.
