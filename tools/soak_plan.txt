# Rush 2049's soak plan: one round through the game's modes and scenes, three minutes with the boot, played on the
# Linux build by a soak (frame_dump.mjs --soak 185, with --args "--players 2"), which fails on a Crash: line, the
# program exiting, or the picture standing still for 10 s, saying whether the game's RAM went on changing (a frozen
# picture) or not (a hang). Its steps are capture_window.ps1's plan steps, plus `<t> random <key>,...`, which presses
# those keys at random until `<t> random off`. It failed v0.1.51 (from the two-player race's car select on, the
# picture black and still, the game's RAM changing) and passes v0.1.78.
#
# A round, one player: Stunt's first arena with Rewind held twice (#257's crash), its pause menu's QUIT, the score
# sheet and GAME OVER's QUIT; Ghost Race on Track 1 with its time limit made 5 s, so it plays to its end, BEST GHOSTS
# and GAME OVER's QUIT (#200's freeze); Practice on Track 1, its QUIT back to SELECT TRACK; Circuit's first race under
# random input and its QUIT. Then two players (--players 2 gives the keyboard's input to port 2 as well): Single Race
# on Track 2 (split screen: #303's frozen picture, and v0.1.51's black one) and Battle under random input, each left
# by its QUIT; then the main menu's cursor moved at random until the time is up. Each race starts with the gas held
# through the countdown; each `shot` is a picture of a GAME OVER menu with QUIT picked, where the round got to.
#
# Times are seconds since the program started; each until waits on the game's RAM and moves the later steps on by its
# wait, so the round keeps step with the game, not the clock. 0x801174B4 is the frame loop's state, a bit a screen
# (src/rewind.cpp): 0x8 the title's attract race, 0x10 the main menu, 0x40 SELECT MODE, 0x80 SELECT TRACK, 0x400000 a
# race, 0x800000 a race's end screens (a score sheet, GAME OVER), 0x2000000 Ghost Race's BEST GHOSTS. SELECT MODE's
# cursor, the low byte of 0x803B469C, holds the mode's own number (0 Single Race, 1 Practice, 2 Ghost Race, 3 Circuit,
# 4 Stunt, 6 Battle; Ghost Race and Circuit only with one player, Battle only with more) and stays where it was left;
# the track is the top byte of 0x8014978C (0 Track 1, 1 Track 2, 14 Stunt's first arena); the pause menu's cursor is
# the low byte of 0x80149D98, QUIT its eighth line (7), then Left to YES. GAME OVER's cursor, the low byte of
# 0x801391E0, holds the number of the item under it, not its line (Stunt's: 0 SCOREBOARD, 1 RACE AGAIN, 3 NEW TRACK,
# 4 NEW CAR, 6 QUIT), and Down from QUIT wraps to the top; Downs pressed blind while the menu comes in were sometimes
# lost, and Space then started a race again. Ghost Race's time limits are a float per track from 0x80111754. Keys
# are the keyboard's defaults (README, Controls): Enter Start, Space A and the gas, Shift B, Backspace Rewind.
